Electron-Electron Scattering

Two electrons approach each other, exchange a virtual photon (the wavy line), and repel. This is the fundamental electromagnetic interaction described by QED. Feynman diagrams turned complex quantum calculations into intuitive visual tools.

The Challenger Investigation

O-Ring Failure at Low Temperature

The Discovery

On January 28, 1986, Space Shuttle Challenger broke apart 73 seconds into flight. Feynman joined the Rogers Commission to investigate.

The Demonstration

In one of science's most dramatic moments, Feynman placed a piece of O-ring rubber in a glass of ice water during a live press conference. After removing it, he showed it had lost its elasticity — proving the rubber couldn't seal properly in cold temperatures.

"For a successful technology, reality must take precedence over public relations, for Nature cannot be fooled."
— Richard Feynman, Challenger Report Appendix
